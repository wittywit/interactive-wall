Connection

Notes

Module 1 serves E1 to E3, module 2 serves E4 to E6, module 3 serves E7 (two channels spare). Mux channel 4 and its terminal J7 stay free. Each module sits behind its middle plate.

Each module cable has 6 conductors: 3V3, GND, SDA, SCL, INT, SHIELD. The shield is tied to GND by a 0R link at the carrier end only. Module 1 goes to J4, module 2 to J5, module 3 to J6.

The carrier is true size (125 x 56 mm) and sits inside the wall. Modules are drawn about 3x. People are scaled models, standing 2 to 3 ft from the wall. Do not ring a plate with ground, that lowers sensitivity.
